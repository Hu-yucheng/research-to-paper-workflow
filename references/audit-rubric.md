# Pre-Submission Audit Rubric

Use this reference for integrated review of a partial or complete manuscript.

## Start with Coverage

List checked and unchecked manuscript sections, figures and tables, references, supplements and full methods, statistics or code, ethics and availability statements, and target-journal instructions. Bound every verdict to this coverage.

## Readiness Verdict

- **Not ready:** one or more P0 issues or foundational evidence is unavailable.
- **Major scientific revision required:** P1 issues prevent a defensible submission.
- **Structurally ready after targeted revision:** scientific chain is defensible; P2 work remains.
- **Language and format pass remaining:** only P3 issues remain in checked material.
- **Ready within checked scope:** no unresolved P0-P3 findings in inspected material; name unchecked items.

## Priority Levels

### P0: Integrity or validity blocker

Factual error, fabricated or unverifiable support, evidence overreach, essential method missing, figure-text contradiction, invented data or mechanism, or an ethics/data-integrity blocker.

### P1: Scientific argument blocker

Mismatch among question, design, results, and conclusion; unsupported causality or mechanism; unaddressed alternatives; inadequate controls, replication, statistics, or scope; unsupported novelty.

### P2: Structure and reader logic

Wrong section or paragraph function, obscured evidence order, mixed Results and Discussion, inconsistent terminology or samples, or abstract/title/conclusion mismatch.

### P3: Language and presentation

Grammar, punctuation, tense, unnecessary words, unclear references, inconsistent abbreviations or units, and non-scientific formatting deviations.

## Finding Format

```markdown
### [P0-P3] Short diagnostic title
- Location:
- Evidence observed:
- Why it matters:
- Required action:
- Verification of closure:
```

Report higher priorities first. Do not bury P0 or P1 findings under line edits.
