# Source Map and Copyright Boundary

## Evidence Levels

1. **Publicly confirmed scope:** title, author, public table of contents, and publisher or author description.
2. **Method synthesis:** a workflow connection inferred from the publicly confirmed domains.
3. **General practice:** operational scientific-writing guidance added for usability, not attributed to a book unless verified.
