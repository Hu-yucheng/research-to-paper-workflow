# Evidence and Claims

Use this reference when the user has literature, observations, data, figures, tables, or preliminary interpretations.

## Evidence Labels

| Label | Test | Permitted wording |
|---|---|---|
| Verified fact | Directly supported by supplied data or a checked source | “showed,” “was measured,” or “the source reports,” within scope |
| Inference | Supported indirectly or conditionally; alternatives remain | “suggests,” “is consistent with,” or “may reflect” |
| Hypothesis | Testable explanation not established by current evidence | “we hypothesize,” “could arise from,” or “requires testing” |
| Unknown | Information needed before evaluation | State the missing observation, analysis, or source |

“Plausible” is not a fifth evidence class. Put it under inference or hypothesis and state why.

## Build the Ledger

| ID | Statement | Status | Evidence | Scope or uncertainty | Next check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Split compound statements when their parts have different status. A measured property trend may be a fact while the proposed molecular cause remains a hypothesis.

## Build the Claim-Evidence-Gap Matrix

| Claim | Supporting evidence | Counterevidence or alternatives | Scope limit | Gap | Discriminating next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For every claim, name the exact evidence, identify alternatives, distinguish association from mechanism or causality, limit scope to tested conditions, and choose the next check most likely to change the conclusion.

## Evidence Checks

- Distinguish independent samples from repeated measurements.
- Do not use “significant” without the relevant analysis.
- Identify the control that isolates the proposed cause.
- State what a technique directly measures versus what is inferred.
- Distinguish direct literature support, indirect analogy, and project hypothesis.
- Preserve negative or null results when they bound the claim.
- Verify figure labels, units, uncertainty, and text agreement.

## Conflict Handling

Report conflicting observations or sources, their verification status, differences in boundary conditions, and the check that could discriminate among explanations.

## Admission to IMRaD Construction

Advance when a claim's evidence anchor, scope, uncertainty, and role in the research question are explicit. Unsupported mechanism language may appear only as a bounded hypothesis with a test, never as a Results fact.
